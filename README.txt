Jupyter Notebooks to analyze 96-well plate growth curve assays

Notebooks are separated by the number of conditions tested in the 96-well plate (1 to 3).

1C = 1 condition tested per plate
2C = 2 conditions tested per plate
3C = 3 conditions tested per plate

Calculate and extract into a CSV file: Final yield, Maximum growth rate, Half saturation constant, and Maximum uptake rate.

Input required: CSV table with time, temperature, and OD, CSV table with well ID and condition (see example files). 
In this assay, it is assumed that border wells are negative growth control.
